Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026
Spotlight respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how data is handled across the Spotlight mobile application and club management platform.
1

About Spotlight

Spotlight is a college event discovery and registration platform. Events are created and managed by authorized college clubs through the Spotlight Club Dashboard. General users use the mobile app to search, browse, and register for campus events.

2

Information We Collect

Account & Profile Data

Full name, email address, phone number, and academic details (USN/Roll number, Branch, Year, and Semester). Password hashes are securely stored if registered manually.

Event Registrations & Teams

Metadata showing which events you join, generated team passkeys, and team member relationships.

Payment & Verification Screenshots

For paid events, we collect your entered UPI Transaction ID (UTR) and image uploads of payment proof screenshots for verification.

3

How We Use Data

We process your information to create your account, process registrations, confirm payments, send internal notifications, combat spam/abuse, and verify academic eligibility for student events.

4

Information Shared With Clubs

When you register for an event, your profile data (Name, USN, Phone, Email), team details, and proof of payment are shared directly with the authorized club organizing that specific event via their Club Dashboard so organizers can verify your entry.

5

Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photo Library: To select and upload screenshots of payment proofs during event registration.
  • Clipboard Access: To copy club UPI IDs and registration passkeys to your clipboard.
  • External App Launch: To open external UPI payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and web links.
6

Data Security & Retention

We use secure JWT authentication and encrypted HTTPS endpoints. We retain account and event registration data while your account remains active as required for college event verification and record-keeping.

7

Account & Data Deletion Request

Spotlight users have full right to request account and data deletion at any time. To request deletion of your account and associated profile data, send an email to spotlightapp.help@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" along with your registered email and USN. Upon verification, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

8
