Spotlight

Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Last Updated: November 19, 2026
Welcome to Spotlight. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spotlight collects, how it is used, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted. It covers the Spotlight Android app, the Spotlight student website and the Spotlight Club Dashboard.
1

About Spotlight

Spotlight is a college event discovery and registration platform. It has three parts:

  • Spotlight App and Student Website: Students browse events and clubs, save events, register individually or in teams, and get a QR entry ticket for each confirmed registration.
  • Spotlight Club Dashboard: Clubs create and manage events, review registrations, check payment transaction IDs and scan entry tickets. A club can sign in only after the Spotlight admin approves its registration.
  • Spotlight Admin Console: Used by the Spotlight admin to review, approve or reject club registrations.
2

Information We Collect

A. Student Account & Profile
  • Full Name and Email Address: To identify your account and your registrations.
  • Phone Number: So the organizing club can reach you about an event you registered for.
  • USN / Roll Number: To identify you as a student and prevent duplicate registrations.
  • Branch, Year and Semester: So clubs know who is attending.
  • Password: If you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name and email address from Google and store no password.
B. Registrations, Teams & Tickets
  • Registrations: The events you register for and their status (pending, confirmed or rejected).
  • Teams: Team names, the team leader and members, and the team passkey.
  • Check-in: The time your entry ticket was scanned at the event.
  • Notifications: In-app messages about your registrations and upcoming events.
C. Payments

Spotlight does not process payments. For paid events you pay the organizing club directly through UPI. We store only the UPI Transaction ID (UTR) you enter, so the club can confirm your payment. We never collect bank account, card or UPI PIN details, and no payment screenshots are uploaded.

D. Club Information

When a club registers, we collect the club name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash, or Google Sign-In instead), department, description, coordinator name and phone number, and optional logo. Clubs can also add a UPI ID and payment QR code image, and event details such as title, description, venue, dates, fee, capacity and banner image. We also keep the status of the club's registration and any reason the admin gives for rejecting it.

E. Stored Only on Your Device

The app and website keep your sign-in session, a cached copy of your profile, tickets and clubs, your saved events, and your light/dark theme choice on your device. Saved events are not sent to our servers. Signing out or clearing app data removes them.

F. Technical Information

Like any website, our servers and hosting providers keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and troubleshooting. Spotlight does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

3

How We Use Your Information

  • Accounts: Signing you in and keeping your session secure.
  • Registrations & Tickets: Registering you for events and teams, issuing QR entry tickets and checking you in.
  • Payment Confirmation: Letting the organizing club match your transaction ID with the payment it received.
  • Notifications: Showing in-app notifications about registration updates and upcoming events. Spotlight does not send push notifications or marketing emails.
  • Club Approval: Reviewing club registrations, and emailing a club once it is approved.
  • Security: Preventing duplicate registrations, misuse and unauthorized access.
4

Who Can See Your Information

Spotlight does not sell, rent or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

The Club Organizing an Event

When you register for an event, the club running it sees your name, email, phone number, USN, branch, year, semester, team name, transaction ID, and your registration and check-in status. Clubs may use this only to run that event.

The Spotlight Admin

The Spotlight admin can see club registration details to approve or reject clubs, and may access platform data for support and moderation.

Service Providers
  • Render: Hosts the Spotlight server.
  • GoDaddy: Hosts the Spotlight database, website and uploaded images.
  • Google: Provides Google Sign-In, and Gmail is used to send club approval emails.

Club logos, event banners and payment QR codes are public images: anyone with the image link can view them.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or by college authorities to protect student safety and platform security.

5

App Permissions

The Spotlight Android app asks only for internet and network access. It does not access your camera, location, contacts, files or photos.

  • Copy to Clipboard: When you tap copy, a club's UPI ID or a team passkey is copied. The app does not read your clipboard.
  • Opening Other Apps: Links can open your UPI payment app, email app or browser.
6

Data Security

  • Encrypted Connections: The app, website and server communicate over HTTPS.
  • Passwords: Stored only as secure hashes, never in plain text.
  • Sessions: Sign-in sessions use signed tokens, and each club can access only its own events and registrations.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

7

Data Retention

We keep your account, registrations and tickets while your account is active, so you and the organizing clubs can see past events. A rejected club registration is kept so the club can apply again, unless the club asks us to delete it. Everything is deleted when you ask us to delete your account (see below).

8

Account & Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete your Spotlight account and data at any time. Students and clubs follow the same steps.

How to Request Deletion:
Email spotlightapp.help@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request", from the email address registered with Spotlight. Students should include their USN; clubs should include the club name.

Once we confirm the request, your account, profile, registrations and team memberships (and, for clubs, the club's events and uploaded images) are permanently deleted within 7 business days.

9

Children's Privacy

Spotlight is meant for college students and clubs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal data, we delete it.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Spotlight changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continuing to use Spotlight after an update means you accept the revised policy.
