# Spotlight Club Dashboard Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 26, 2026**

Spotlight ("Spotlight", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of clubs and organizations using the Spotlight Club Dashboard. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect that information, and how participant information made available through the Dashboard must be handled.

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to clubs, organizers, and organizations that use the Spotlight Club Dashboard to create, publish, and manage events.

By accessing or using the Spotlight Club Dashboard, the club or organization acknowledges the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. About the Spotlight Club Dashboard

The Spotlight Club Dashboard allows authorized clubs and organizations to create, publish, and manage events that are displayed to users of the Spotlight mobile application.

Clubs use the Dashboard to:
* Create and manage events (solo or team-based).
* Set event names, descriptions, dates, times, capacity limits, deadlines, and locations/venues.
* Upload event posters, banner graphics, and organization logos.
* Configure payment details (UPI IDs and UPI QR codes) for paid events.
* Review, manage, approve, or deny participant registrations.
* Access registration details of users who sign up for their events.
* Verify participant payment proofs (Transaction IDs and screenshot uploads).
* Update or cancel previously published events.

Events published through the Dashboard are visible to all users of the Spotlight mobile application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect From Clubs

To create and maintain your club profile on Spotlight and operate the Dashboard, we collect and store:
* **Club Account Details:** Club Name, Official Email Address, and authentication credentials.
* **Account Verification (Registration Keys):** To prevent unauthorized club registrations, new club accounts are activated using a unique single-use **Registration Key** generated by Spotlight administrators.
* **Financial Details:** For paid events, we store the club's **UPI ID** and **UPI QR Code URL** to display to users during event checkout in the mobile app.
* **Content and Assets:** Custom club logos, event posters, banner graphics, and description texts uploaded during event creation.
* **Credentials:** Hashed versions of passwords for legacy direct login support, and system tokens created during active sessions.

---

## 3. Club Authentication and Account Security

Access to the Spotlight Club Dashboard requires verification to protect event configurations and participant rosters.

* **Third-Party Authentication (Clerk):** We integrate with **Clerk** to provide admin sign-in, verify session state, and secure access to the Club Dashboard. When logging in, authentication status is verified using secure Clerk JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
* **Access Control:** The club is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of its login credentials. Dashboard accounts should only be accessed by authorized club members, coordinators, or officers.
* **Responsibility:** The club is responsible for all activity performed through its account. The club must immediately notify Spotlight support of any suspected unauthorized access or token leakage.

---

## 4. Event Information

When a club creates an event through the Dashboard, Spotlight collects and stores all related event information, including:
* Event name, category, description, date/time, and physical venue/location.
* Entry fee pricing, participant capacity limits, and registration deadlines.
* Custom event banners, posters, and rules.
* Registration details (whether the event allows solo entries or requires teams of specific sizes).

Once an event is published, this information is public and visible to all users of the Spotlight mobile application. Clubs must ensure they have the necessary intellectual property rights, licenses, or permissions for any graphics, logos, posters, or texts they upload.

---

## 5. Participant Information Available to Clubs

When a Spotlight user registers for your event, their registration data is made available to you through the Spotlight Club Dashboard so that you can verify eligibility and manage entry. 

This participant data includes:
* **Academic & Profile Identifiers:** Name, College USN / Roll Number, Branch, Year of study, Semester, Email address, and Phone number.
* **Team Structure:** Team name, generated team passkeys, and team member listings.
* **Payment Validation Data (For Paid Events):** The participant's input **UPI Transaction ID (UTR)** and a **screenshot proof of payment** uploaded by the user.

Dashboard access is restricted so that clubs can only view participant information for events they are directly responsible for organizing. You do not have access to registrations of unrelated clubs.

---

## 6. How Clubs Must Handle Participant Information

Participant information accessed through the Spotlight Club Dashboard is sensitive and must be handled in accordance with strict guidelines:

* **Legitimate Use Only:** Clubs must use participant information *only* for legitimate purposes connected with organizing, verifying, communicating updates for, and executing the specific event for which the user registered.
* **No Misuse or Marketing:** Clubs must not use participant contact details (phone, email) for unrelated marketing, advertisements, or spam.
* **No Unauthorized Sharing:** Clubs must not distribute, sell, share, or lease participant information to external third parties.
* **Exported Data Security:** If a club exports, downloads, or prints participant rosters from the Dashboard, the club administrators are solely responsible for protecting that data on their personal devices and must delete it securely after the event concludes.

---

## 7. How We Use Club Information

We use the information collected from clubs to:
* Authenticate and authorize administrators on the Dashboard.
* Maintain active, secure club profiles on our database.
* Allow the creation, scheduling, and publication of events.
* Display events, logos, and UPI payment details within the mobile app.
* Process registration actions (approvals/denials) initiated by the club.
* Manage platform security, trace bugs, and monitor API traffic.
* Provide user support and comply with regulatory requirements.

We do not sell club or participant personal information.

---

## 8. How We Share Information (Third-Party Services)

We do not sell or rent club account details or participant listings. We share or host information with the following service providers to run the platform:

* **Authentication & Session Management:**
  * **Clerk Auth:** Used to run authentication, sign-up, sign-in, and verification workflows for club accounts.
* **Cloud Storage Infrastructure:**
  * **Supabase Storage:** All uploaded club logos, event posters/banners, and user payment screenshots are stored on Supabase cloud storage buckets (`spotlight-images`).
* **Database & Hosting Provider:**
  * Our servers run on cloud infrastructure utilizing PostgreSQL databases to store records of clubs, events, registrations, and activation keys.
* **Legal Compliance:**
  * We may disclose info where required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users, platform integrity, or to investigate platform abuse.

---

## 9. Data Storage and Security

We implement server-side access controls, HTTPS transport encryption, and secure JWT checks. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. 

Clubs play an essential role in securing the ecosystem:
* Restrict login access only to trusted executive coordinators.
* Do not share active session keys or passwords.
* Immediately notify Spotlight support if an unauthorized coordinator accesses the Dashboard.

---

## 10. Data Retention

We retain club account profiles, event histories, and associated registration records to maintain platform audit history, verify past event entries, and support active accounts.
* Event logs and rosters remain on the server to allow users to review their ticket history.
* When a club account is permanently deactivated, associated event details may be archived or anonymized.

---

## 11. Club Account and Data Deletion

A club may request deactivation or deletion of its Spotlight club profile by contacting the Spotlight support team through the official support channel. 

Upon deactivation:
* The club will lose access to the Spotlight Club Dashboard.
* Future scheduled events associated with the club will be withdrawn from the mobile app.
* Historical event statistics and public records may be archived or anonymized in accordance with backup policies.

---

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to align with new features, software packages, or security policies. We will notify clubs of major modifications through a notification banner in the Club Dashboard or via email updates.
